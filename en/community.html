1. Purpose

These Guidelines set out how players are expected to behave in Dungeon Core Summoner, what we do about behaviour that falls short, and how to challenge a decision you think is wrong. They exist so that enforcement is consistent and predictable rather than arbitrary.

Anything not covered here is handled under the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy and applicable law.

2. Your rights as a player

  • You will be told, in-game, when an enforcement action has been applied to your account, and which category of these Guidelines it relates to.
  • You may appeal any enforcement action (section 9).
  • You may contact support about any decision, and expect a substantive answer.
  • You may report other players and expect reports to be reviewed.
  • You may delete your account at any time under Settings → Delete Account.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Play on your own account, with your own information. Do not use anyone else’s.
  • Keep your login details private. You are responsible for what happens on your account, including where you let someone else use it.
  • Do not share, sell, buy, rent or transfer accounts or items. We do not recognise such transfers, and we cannot help with losses or scams that follow from them.
  • Do not post another person’s private information, and be careful with your own.
  • Report bugs and exploits to us rather than using them or passing them on.
  • Do not obstruct or impersonate our staff.

4. Naming standards

These standards apply to every name and image you choose, including your display name (which is public on leaderboards), your guild name and any guild description or profile image.

You may not use a name or image that:

  1. is offensive, distressing or designed to disturb other players;
  2. contains profanity, slurs, sexual, obscene or violent content;
  3. attacks or demeans a country, region, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any protected group;
  4. infringes another party’s trademark, copyright or other rights;
  5. impersonates LEEDIAN, our staff, our partners, or any other real person;
  6. could be mistaken for an official role — for example “Admin”, “GM”, “Moderator”, “Support”, “Staff”, “NPC” or “System”;
  7. advertises, or solicits or facilitates the real-money sale of accounts, currency or items;
  8. promotes or glorifies criminal, extremist or antisocial organisations or individuals; or
  9. advertises any product, service or website, whether commercial or not.

Where a name breaches these standards we will normally change it to a system-assigned name rather than ban the account. Any consequence of that change — loss of a name you wanted, or of recognition on the leaderboard — is not something we can compensate. Repeatedly setting a breaching name escalates under section 8.

5. Conduct towards other players

Do not:

  • harass, bully, threaten, stalk or intimidate another player;
  • post hateful content directed at a person or a protected group;
  • post sexual content, or any content sexualising a minor — this results in an immediate permanent ban and, where applicable, a report to the authorities;
  • deceive or defraud another player, including false trades, false giveaways and phishing for account details;
  • spam, flood or advertise; or
  • encourage or coordinate any of the above.

6. Fair play

Do not:

  • use cheats, trainers, bots, macros, auto-clickers, modified clients, memory or save editors, packet manipulation or any unauthorised third-party program;
  • exploit a bug, glitch or unintended behaviour, or keep quiet about one you have found and benefited from;
  • use multiple accounts to gain an unfair advantage, manipulate leaderboards, or evade an enforcement action;
  • engage in real-money trading — buying, selling or advertising accounts, Crystals, Gold or items for money or off-platform value; or
  • attempt to gain unauthorised access to our systems or another player’s account, or to disrupt, overload or attack the Service.

Currency, items and rankings obtained through any of the above will be removed or reset in addition to any other action.

9. Reporting and appeals

Reporting another player

Use the Report option next to a player’s name in the leaderboard or guild, or email dcs.game.support@gmail.com with the player’s display name, what happened, and a screenshot if you have one. We aim to review reports within 24 hours. We will not tell you what action was taken against another player, as that is their personal data.

You can also block a player to stop seeing their content. Reports made in bad faith, or in volume to harass someone, are themselves a breach of these Guidelines.

Appealing a decision about your account

  • Appeal in-game under Settings → Support, or by email to dcs.game.support@gmail.com.
  • You must appeal within 15 days of the action being applied.
  • We will decide within 15 days of receiving your appeal and tell you the outcome and the reason.
  • Include your account identifier and anything you want us to consider.

If we find we were wrong, we will reverse the action and restore what was removed where we can. Where an action was justified, we do not compensate for time lost during a suspension, and unused Crystals on a permanently banned account are not refunded, to the extent permitted by law.

10. Inactive and guest accounts

  • An account with no login for 12 months may be treated as inactive, and its data may be deleted as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • A guest account exists only on the device that created it. If you uninstall, change device or clear app data, its progress and any Crystals on it may be permanently lost. Link an email address or a third-party sign-in to protect it — especially before buying anything.

11. Changes to these Guidelines

We may update these Guidelines to keep the Game safe and fair. We will give notice in-game or on this page at least 7 days before a change takes effect, or at least 30 days where the change is materially adverse to players. The version number and effective date above will change.
