1. Purpose and scope

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you play Dungeon Core Summoner (the “Game”), why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Game on all platforms and to any related support channel we operate.

We have deliberately kept the Game’s data footprint small. In particular:

  • We do not display advertising and we use no advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or attribution SDKs. The basic game statistics we collect (see section 2) go only to our own database.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use the device advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We do not collect your precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.

2. What we collect

Account data

  • Account identifier — a randomly generated user ID created when you first play. If you play as a guest, this is the only identifier we hold for you and it is not linked to your real identity.
  • Email address — only if you choose to link one to protect your progress.
  • Password — if you set one, it is stored only as a salted cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Third-party sign-in identifier — if you sign in with a supported provider, we receive the account identifier that provider gives us. We do not receive your password.
  • Sign-in timestamps — the time of your most recent login, used for support, daily rewards and inactive-account handling.

Profile and gameplay data

  • Display name — the name you choose. Note this is public: it is visible to other players on leaderboards and in guilds.
  • Save data — your progress, chapter and wave records, unit roster, formation, research, archive completion, and Crystal and Gold balances, synchronised so you can recover your account on a new device.
  • Ranking data — your best wave, highest chapter and best Endless wave, shown publicly on leaderboards alongside your display name.
  • Guild data — the guild you belong to and your activity within it.

Purchase data

  • Transaction records — the fact that a purchase occurred, the product bought, and the receipt identifier issued by Apple or Google, so we can grant Crystals, verify purchases and handle support and refunds.

WE NEVER RECEIVE OR STORE YOUR CARD NUMBER, BANK DETAILS OR BILLING ADDRESS. ALL PAYMENTS ARE PROCESSED BY APPLE OR GOOGLE UNDER THEIR OWN PRIVACY POLICIES.

Technical data

  • Server log data — when the Game contacts our servers, our hosting provider records the request, the time and the originating IP address. This is used for security, abuse prevention and diagnosing faults.
  • Gameplay events — the Game records what happens in play, such as starting and finishing a battle (chapter, mode, result and waves reached), summoning, shop purchases, levelling up or starring up a summon, claiming daily rewards, missions and achievements, finishing the tutorial, and running short of a currency. Each event carries your account identifier, a random session identifier created each time the Game starts, the time, the app version and the platform (for example iOS).
  • Error reports — when the Game hits a script error, it sends the error message, the place in the Game’s code where it happened, how many times it repeated, the app version and the platform, linked to your account identifier. Error reports never contain your save data, your email address or anything you have typed.

Gameplay events and error reports are sent to our own database (see section 5), not to an analytics company, and are never used for advertising.

Notifications

The Game can send you reminders (for example, when your energy is full or a daily reward is ready). These are scheduled on your device by the operating system. They do not involve a push server, your account, or any transmission of data to us. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

4. Legal bases (EEA and UK players)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to create your account, save your progress, run leaderboards and guilds, and deliver purchases.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Service secure, prevent cheating and fraud, diagnose faults, fix bugs, and understand how the Game is played so we can balance and improve it. We balance these against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — to keep tax and transaction records and to respond to lawful requests.
  • Consent — where we ask for it specifically. You may withdraw consent at any time.

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the following categories of recipient, and only as far as needed:

Your display name and ranking data are public within the Game and visible to other players. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as your display name.

6. International transfers

Our servers are located in Singapore (Supabase, hosted on Amazon Web Services, region ap-southeast-1). If you play from outside that region, your data will be transferred there. Where data leaves the EEA or the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. You can request a copy of the relevant safeguards from dcs.game.support@gmail.com.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and save data — while your account is active, and for up to 12 months after it becomes inactive, after which it may be deleted.
  • Deleted accounts — removed from our live systems promptly and purged from backups within 30 days.
  • Purchase records — retained as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically 7 years, even after account deletion.
  • Enforcement records — where an account was permanently banned, we may retain the minimum data needed to enforce that ban.
  • Server logs — retained for a short period for security, then deleted.
  • Gameplay events and error reports — kept for 12 months, then deleted. They are also deleted at once, together with everything else, when you delete your account.

8. Your rights and controls

Right of access

You can ask us to confirm what personal data we hold about you and to receive a copy.

Correction

You can correct your display name in-game at any time. For anything else, contact us and we will correct inaccurate data.

Deletion

You can delete your account and its data yourself, at any time, from Settings → Delete Account in the Game. This is immediate and permanent. You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to dcs.game.support@gmail.com.

Data portability

You can ask for the data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

Restriction and objection

You can ask us to restrict processing, or object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds.

Withdrawing consent

Where we process on the basis of consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it.

How to exercise your rights

Email dcs.game.support@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first, usually by asking you to write from the email address linked to the account or to supply your in-game account identifier. We do not charge for this unless a request is manifestly excessive.

If you are in the EEA or the UK you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in California, you have the rights to know, delete, correct, and to not be discriminated against for exercising them; we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

9. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact dcs.game.support@gmail.com and we will delete the account and its data. A parent or guardian may contact us to review, correct or delete data relating to their child.

10. Security

We protect your data with measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), encryption at rest, hashed passwords, access controls limiting staff access to what their role requires, and row-level database rules so one player cannot read another’s save data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach occurs that is likely to result in a high risk to your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version number and effective date above will change, and earlier versions remain available on request. If a change is material we will give notice in-game at least 30 days before it takes effect, and where the law requires it, ask for your consent.
